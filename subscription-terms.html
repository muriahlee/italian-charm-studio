Price

The first month is $49.99 and includes the starter bracelet in its presentation box plus 2 charms. Every month after is $24.99. The minimum is 3 months. Gift lengths of 3, 6, and 12 months add the first-month price to the later monthly price for each month after the first. Gold tone and silver tone are the same price. Plus applicable sales tax.

What arrives

The first month: the starter bracelet, a champagne presentation box with a black velvet raised insert, the first two charms, a numbered positivity card, and a sticker. Every month after: two new charms, a numbered positivity card, a sticker, and a black keepsake jewelry box tied with Fiorvelle Rose ribbon. This template does not promise a delivery date, and it does not quote shipping.

The 3-month minimum

The membership is a 3-month minimum. This prototype does not take payment. A live membership will need account controls for what happens after the third month. Those controls are not written here, and they are not a delivery promise.

Billing

Future billing is intended to run through Shopify. This site does not collect card numbers.
