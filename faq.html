How do Italian charm bracelets work?

The links are the bracelet. A decorative link replaces a plain one. Nothing hangs from a separate chain. This preview uses concept illustrations, not finished product photographs.

How do I determine my bracelet size?

The Mood Bar uses a sizing estimate: wrist in millimetres, plus 10mm of comfort, divided by 9.5mm, then rounded. A 6.5 inch wrist comes to 18 links. That estimate is not a supplier-certified measurement.

Can I change my charms myself?

Yes, with the charm-changing tool. Open the link beside the one you want to change, slide it out, and slide another 9mm link in. The measured length stays the same.

Are gold and silver links interchangeable?

They are the same link size in this design, and the same price. Mixing finishes on one bracelet is a choice you can make in the studio later. This preview builds one finish at a time.

Do I need to purchase a starter bracelet first?
