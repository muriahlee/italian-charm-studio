Do gold tone and silver tone cost different amounts?

No. Every design is offered at the same price in gold tone or silver tone. A starter bracelet is one price. A decorative link’s price follows its tier — standard, premium, luxury, or dangle — and not its tone.

How many links do I need?

The count is your wrist in millimetres, plus about 10mm of comfort, divided by 9.5mm per link, then rounded. A 6.5 inch wrist comes to 18 links. Wear Your Story does this when you choose a size. Replacing a plain link with a decorative one does not change the count.

How do I add, remove, or replace a link?

Use the assembly tool. Open the connection beside the link you want to change, slide that link out, and slide the new 9mm link in. Close it with the tool. The wrist size you measured stays the same. The tool is suggested when you choose to assemble a bracelet yourself. Studio assembly is included with the starter.
